# Theological Review & Discernment Skill

Use this skill to conduct thoughtful, context-aware theological audits of code, prompts, character personas, or scripture metadata.

## Core Philosophy: Discernment over Brittle Automation

Theology is organic, nuanced, and relational. It cannot be reduced to simplistic keyword searches, regex pattern-matching, or arbitrary numerical scores.

Instead of testing for superficial phrases or slogans, this skill equips an agent to read [THEOLOGY.md](file:///usr/local/google/home/markwell/personal_dev/bible/THEOLOGY.md) (The Gospel Coalition Preamble, Confessional Statement, and Theological Vision for Ministry) and apply mature biblical and pastoral discernment.

## The Review Process

1. **Read & Absorb THEOLOGY.md**:
   - Understand the dual-horizon hermeneutic (reading "along" redemptive history and "across" systematic loci).
   - Recognize the gospel's unique third way: distinct from both **legalistic moralism** ("I obey, therefore I am accepted") and **moral relativism / antinomianism** ("I am free to live as I please"). The gospel principle: *"I am accepted through Christ, therefore I obey."*

2. **Examine Target Content for Key Theological Dimensions**:
   - **Christological Teleology**: Does the content point to, prepare for, or flow from Jesus Christ as the climax of redemptive history?
   - **Gospel Grounding**: Is Christian obedience and holiness motivated by gratitude for free justification and empowered by the Holy Spirit, or does it slip into merit-earning or moral relativism?
   - **Canonical Horizon & Realism**: Are biblical characters portrayed with historical honesty—neither as sanitized, marble statues of self-righteous virtue nor as cynical caricatures, but as fallen human beings walking in genuine, Spirit-wrought faith in God's promises?
   - **Whole-Life Faith**: Does the application reflect the breadth of TGC's vision (worship, evangelism, counter-cultural community, integration of faith and work, and the doing of justice and mercy)?

3. **Produce a Qualitative Theological Assessment**:
   Provide constructive, qualitative feedback:
   - **Theological Strengths**: Clear Gospel markers, canonical alignment, reverent tone.
   - **Nuances & Potential Blind Spots**: Areas that lean toward either legalism or antinomian license, or lack Christ-centered grounding.
   - **Recommendations**: Concrete, actionable guidance for prompt authors or developers.
